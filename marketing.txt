PART 3: EXCHANGE LISTING STRATEGY (NO LIQUIDITY POOL)
================================================================================

Your approach: List directly on a centralized exchange (CEX). No PancakeSwap,
no DEX liquidity pool. The exchange handles the order book and market making.

[HOW DIRECT CEX LISTING WORKS]
  1. You apply to the exchange with your project details
  2. Exchange reviews: product, team, tokenomics, community, revenue model
  3. If accepted, you pay a listing fee
  4. Exchange creates the trading pair (TER/USDT, TER/BNB)
  5. They handle the order book — you don't provide liquidity
  6. Some exchanges assign their own market makers

[BEST EXCHANGES FOR DIRECT LISTING (EASIEST → HARDEST)]

  TIER 1 — Easiest to list, lowest fees ($2K-$10K)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • MEXC Global
    - Very BNB Chain friendly, lists hundreds of new tokens monthly
    - Fast review process (1-2 weeks)
    - They have their own market makers
    - Listing fee: ~$2K-$5K
    - Apply: mexc.com/listing

  • LBank
    - Known for listing early-stage projects
    - Has a "LaunchPad" for new tokens
    - Listing fee: ~$3K-$8K
    - Good for initial visibility

  • BitMart
    - Easy listing process
    - Good volume for small-cap tokens
    - Listing fee: ~$5K-$10K

  TIER 2 — Moderate difficulty ($10K-$30K)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────
  • Gate.io
    - Strong volume, respected exchange
    - Has "Startup" program for new projects
    - Listing fee: ~$10K-$20K
    - They require basic audit report

  • Bitget
    - Fast-growing exchange, actively listing
    - Has a "Launchpool" program
    - Listing fee: ~$15K-$25K

  • Phemex
    - Growing exchange with good BSC support
    - Listing fee: ~$10K-$20K

  TIER 3 — Higher barrier ($30K-$100K+)
  ──────────────────────────────────────
  • KuCoin
    - Large user base, community voting helps
    - Listing fee: ~$30K-$50K
    - Requires audit + strong community metrics

  • Bybit
    - Very popular, good for BSC tokens
    - Listing fee: ~$50K-$80K

  • Binance (ultimate goal)
    - Apply via binance.com/en/my/coin-apply
    - No set listing fee, but expects strong metrics
    - Getting on Binance Alpha first helps massively

[WHAT EXCHANGES WANT FROM YOU]
  ✓ Live product (teron.io — you have this)
  ✓ Verified smart contract on BscScan
  ✓ Revenue model (you have dynamic income from premium features)
  ✓ Team info (at least partial doxxing or KYC with exchange)
  ✓ Tokenomics document (supply, distribution, utility)
  ✓ Community metrics (X followers, Telegram members, active users)
  ✓ Basic audit report (Certik Skynet, Hacken, or SolidProof — $2K-$5K)
  ✓ Marketing plan showing growth trajectory

[YOUR ADVANTAGE: DYNAMIC INCOME MODEL]
  Teron generates real revenue from:
  • BscScan verification fees (paid in BNB per token)
  • On-chain metadata fees (paid in BNB per token)
  • Future premium features
  
  This is MASSIVE for exchange listing because:
  → Most projects have zero revenue. You have a working product with income.
  → Exchanges love "revenue-generating protocol" narrative
  → It proves the token has real utility, not just speculation
  → Frame it as: "Teron is a revenue-generating Web3 SaaS platform"
